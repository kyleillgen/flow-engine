Flow Engine

Rhythm micro-games for the gaps while code builds. They find your tempo, never end and never get harder. Six cartridges, one button: Space or tap to play, F for Fidget, where every press lands, and ` for chill, normal or intense.

Space

Cozy neon space: little aliens, a glowing jellyfish, moons, a moon garden, a whale and the stars.

NovaFire as each diver drops into your sights TidePulse while the hoop's inner ring is gold SkipTap as Bobo lands on each moon BloomTap as each seed touches the soil HumTap as each star krill reaches her StarTap as the thread reaches each star

Arcade

A late-night arcade cabinet: a brick-breaker, a maze, a highway, a rock field and a claw machine, on one button.

BonkTap as Pip lands on Slab LampTap as Kip reaches a lantern to light the way HopTap as the gap reaches the coin RocksTap as each Grump touches the ring ClawTap as Mitt stops over the wiggling plushie

Cozy

A moonlit garden: tea that blooms, a glowing axolotl's pond, a cloud bakery, lanterns for a moon moth, and Tidelight.

SteepTap as a cup passes under the spout RippleTap as a drop meets the water FrostTap as each mark comes round to the nozzle LullTap as a lantern reaches Tibbet TidePulse while the hoop's inner ring is gold

Lo-fi Cafe

A café at night: rain on the window, a warm lamp, latte art, steam off the cups and Biscuit the sleepy cat.

PourTap as each cup passes under the pitcher RainTap as each drop touches the sill PawTap as the fish swings into Biscuit's paw

Hunt

A woods lodge at dusk: deer, birds, a roosting turkey and a gallery booth. The sights glide in by themselves; timing is the whole game.

LeapTap at the top of each deer's leap FlushTap as each bird hangs at the top RoostTap as the turkey pops out RangeTap as each target crosses the brass mark

Deep Sea

A bioluminescent abyss: an anglerfish's lantern, a siphonophore's chain of light, a little submersible's headlamp and a squid that talks in flashes.

LureGlow as each little one touches the lantern ChainTap as the light reaches the new bell LampSnap as the headlamp finds each creature EchoFlash back as the far squid's light reaches you
