Flow Engine

Rhythm micro-games for the gaps while code builds. They find your tempo, never end and never get harder. Space or tap to play, hold and let go for a trick shot, F to switch between Flow and Fidget.

FlowTap a steady rhythm and fly the ribbon through the pillars. OrbitFling the ball off its bumper as it crosses the lit arrow. FlipperOne button, both flippers. Flip as the ball lands. RicochetKick off each wall as the ball reaches it and climb the shaft.
